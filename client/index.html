Home

Train the Body. Discipline the Mind. Strengthen the Spirit

Welcome to the Martial Arts Karate Academy, NIT Hamirpur — a community built on discipline, courage, self-defence and continuous growth. Established in May 2018, the Academy was created to bring martial arts into the life of NIT Hamirpur students, providing them with an environment where physical fitness meets mental strength and engineering education meets sporting discipline. Here, we don't simply learn how to fight. We learn how to stand strong, stay disciplined, respect others and face challenges with confidence. From beginners taking their first steps on the mat to experienced cadets competing in tournaments, every journey begins with one thing — the willingness to learn. Step onto the mat. Find your strength. Build your legacy.

ABOUT THE ACADEMY

More Than Karate. A Way of Life.

The Martial Arts Karate Academy at NIT Hamirpur is a registered academy affiliated with the Karate Association of Himachal Pradesh and the Karate Association of India. It is an integral part of the sports and student activities at NIT Hamirpur. The Academy was introduced in May 2018 by Mr. Gaurav Sharma, a senior black belt associated with the World Taekwondo Federation and Karate Association of India, and an Electrical Engineering student of NIT Hamirpur from the 2016–2020 batch. He served as the Academy's first coordinator and instructor. The Academy was established with a simple but powerful purpose: To make martial arts a medium for self-defence, confidence building, fitness and personal growth. Alongside regular academic life, training gives students an opportunity to step away from academic pressure, challenge themselves physically and mentally, and develop habits that stay with them far beyond the dojo.

Our Vision

We envision a community of students who are physically capable, mentally resilient and confident enough to protect themselves and others. A major focus of the Academy is self-defence and empowerment, particularly for women, helping students develop awareness, confidence and practical survival skills.

Our Belief

Karate is not only about punches, kicks or winning competitions. It is about discipline when nobody is watching, courage when things become difficult, humility after victory and the strength to stand up after failure.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Built Through Discipline. Proven Through Performance.

The Academy's journey has been marked by participation, competition and continuous improvement.

2018

A Strong Beginning

At the South Asian Hakuakai Karate Championship 2018, six NIT Hamirpur students participated and collectively secured: 2 Gold • 3 Silver • 2 Bronze The medals were won across Kata and Kumite events. This early success established the Academy as a serious martial arts community within NIT Hamirpur.

2018

SPORTS MEET — LALKAAR

During the 2018 Sports Meet, Academy trainees presented demonstrations showcasing: Martial arts techniques Karate movements Self-defence techniques Women's self-defence techniques The demonstration brought martial arts beyond competition and showed its practical role in confidence and self-protection.

2023

KUMITE SUCCESS

Academy leadership and cadet profiles also document competitive achievements, including a Gold Medal in the Kumite competition on 12 March 2023. The same profile records training and supervision of more than 60 cadets and involvement in self-defence camps and institute parades.

2025-26

CONTINUING THE TRADITION

The Academy continues to organise competitive and developmental activities. Recent public updates include: Kumite Fight Tournament — March 2025 Belt-level achievements and promotions Republic Day parade participation Freshers recruitment and induction Yoga and wellness activities Kumite Fight Tournament — March 2026 Farewell and legacy events for graduating cadets These activities reflect an Academy that continues to evolve beyond traditional training.

WHAT WE DO

TRAIN. DEFEND. COMPETE. LEAD.

The Academy combines martial arts training with fitness, self-defence, competition and personal development.

🥋 KARATE TRAINING

Learn the fundamentals and advanced aspects of karate through structured practice.
Training focuses on:

• Stances
• Blocks
• Strikes
• Kicks
• Movement
• Kata
• Kumite
• Timing
• Control
• Technique

🛡️ SELF-DEFENCE

Martial arts should be practical. Our training focuses on developing awareness, confidence and the ability to respond effectively in difficult situations. Special emphasis is placed on self-defence and women's empowerment.

⚡ FITNESS & CONDITIONING

Martial arts demands a strong body.

Training helps cadets develop:

• Strength
• Speed
• Agility
• Flexibility
• Balance
• Stamina
• Coordination

🏆 COMPETITIONS

Cadets get opportunities to test their skills through competitive events and tournaments. The Academy organises activities such as Kumite competitions where students can challenge themselves in a controlled sporting environment.

🎖️ DRILL & PARADES

Discipline extends beyond the dojo.

Academy cadets have participated in institute events and national-day parades, developing coordination, discipline and team spirit. Recent Academy updates also highlight Republic Day participation.

🧘 WELLNESS

Strength is not only physical.

The Academy also participates in activities promoting physical and mental well-being, including yoga and wellness initiatives.

🤝 COMMUNITY & LEADERSHIP

Senior cadets help guide juniors, organise activities and contribute to the Academy's functioning.

This creates an environment where cadets don't just become better athletes — they become better teammates and leaders.

ALUMNI

Once a Cadet. Always a Part of the Academy.

Graduation may take a cadet away from the NIT Hamirpur campus, but it does not erase the connection built on the mat.
Over the years, cadets have moved into different fields and different parts of the world, carrying with them the lessons they learned through martial arts:

Discipline. Courage. Resilience. Respect.

Our alumni are an important part of the Academy's story.

They are the seniors who once trained beside us, the mentors who guided us and the people who helped shape the culture that exists today.
